HOW IT WORKS

A clearer path to self-health checks.

Inspired by NASA's challenge: gather measurements, understand trends, and support decisions to contact qualified crew medical personnel.

01✎

Record

Enter a check-in through straightforward controls for heart rate, oxygen, sleep, temperature and additional indicators.

02⌁

Visualize

See current readings and interactive charts. Our initial history is clearly labeled as simulated, never real telemetry.

03✳

Review & act

Read practical measurement reminders and request a crew medical review. Clinical guidance will require validation.
